BENGALURU OFFICE ARRIVAL GUIDE
=============================

DOCUMENT INFORMATION
Organization: Northstar Learning Services (fictional)
Document ID: WPL-002
Version: 1.0
Effective from: 1 September 2026
Owner: Workplace Services
Status: Current
Classification: Fictional classroom material

1. PURPOSE
Explain first-day reception, temporary passes, and equipment-collection visits at the Bengaluru office.

2. SCOPE AND APPLICABILITY
Permanent employees and interns attending the Bengaluru office as new joiners or for an approved onboarding visit. These entry instructions do not apply to Pune. This document does not establish a street address, parking entitlement, or shuttle timetable.

3. POLICY GUIDANCE

3.1 BEFORE THE VISIT
Confirm that Bengaluru is the actual location for this visit and confirm the approved work arrangement. An employee assigned to Bengaluru may still have an approved remote start. A remote employee should not travel to the office solely because the assigned office is shown on a joining record.

Locate the joining invitation and the identity-document instructions in the secure joining communication. If the physical address or arrival contact is missing, ask Workplace Services through the approved channel. Do not infer these details from a map listing or a different company's office.

3.2 FIRST SCHEDULED OFFICE DAY
Report to reception at 9:30 AM unless the joining invitation specifies a different time. Bring the joining invitation and the identity document specified in the secure communication. Reception verifies entry and directs the employee to the induction room.

The reception time is not necessarily the welcome-session start time. If an invitation requests a different arrival time, follow that invitation. If the invitation is unclear, ask Workplace Services to confirm rather than choosing the Pune reporting time.

3.3 BADGES AND DELAYED ARRIVAL
Workplace Services issues a temporary access pass if the permanent badge is not ready. Follow the pass conditions supplied at reception. Do not borrow a colleague's badge or enter behind another person without verification.

If delayed, notify the manager and Workplace Services through the established channel and ask for revised arrival instructions. A late arrival does not guarantee that the original induction session can be joined. People Operations confirms any replacement orientation arrangement.

3.4 EQUIPMENT-COLLECTION VISITS
An employee visiting only to collect a laptop must first receive IT's ready-for-collection confirmation. Confirm that collection is at Bengaluru, then ask Workplace Services about entry instructions for that visit. A general first-day invitation is not proof that equipment is ready.

If IT changes the collection office to Pune, use the Pune visitor-registration process. Do not apply Bengaluru reception procedures to another location simply because Bengaluru remains the assigned office.

3.5 ACCESS REQUIREMENTS
Tell Workplace Services privately if an adjustment to the entry or workspace arrangement is needed. Ask what details and lead time are required; no universal adjustment fulfilment deadline is specified. Share only necessary information through the confidential route.

3.6 VALID MOCK EXAMPLES
- Bengaluru arrival case WPL-BLR-2026-09041: first scheduled office day 15 September 2026, joining invitation specifies 11:00 AM. The valid arrival time is 11:00 AM, not the default 9:30 AM.
- Bengaluru equipment visit WPL-BLR-2026-09042: employee has IT ready-for-collection confirmation for Bengaluru but no permanent badge. Workplace Services may issue a temporary pass after verification; badge borrowing is invalid.
- A Bengaluru guide answer is invalid when the employee later corrects the actual visit location to Pune. Re-evaluate with the corrected location.

4. SUPPORT AND ESCALATION
Workplace Services handles reception and badges. People Operations handles orientation invitations. IT Support confirms equipment readiness, and the manager coordinates attendance changes.

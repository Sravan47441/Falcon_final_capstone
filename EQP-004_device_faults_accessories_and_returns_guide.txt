1. PURPOSE
Describe how to report equipment problems, request optional accessories, and return a device when IT instructs the employee to do so.

2. SCOPE AND APPLICABILITY
Company-managed equipment issued to permanent employees and interns in Bengaluru or Pune, including approved remote workers. This guide does not specify replacement eligibility, employee liability, repair prices, or an automatic accessory entitlement.

3. POLICY GUIDANCE

3.1 REPORT A FAULT
Use the IT service portal to describe the affected item, symptoms, time first observed, work impact, and any existing request reference. Supply an asset reference only through the approved support channel. Use a non-sensitive summary when asking a general assistant for guidance.

Distinguish a missing charger from a laptop that cannot start, an account activation error, or a request for a better model. These cases may need different handling. Follow only the troubleshooting steps supplied by authorized IT staff. Do not disassemble equipment, disable controls, or arrange an external repair without IT approval.

3.2 DAMAGE OR LOSS
Report loss or suspected theft promptly through the approved IT support route and notify the manager. IT determines the necessary security and equipment response. Do not claim that an account has been disabled or a device remotely wiped unless the authorized team confirms it.

For visible damage on receipt, retain the delivery reference and provide evidence through the channel IT requests. The collection does not decide whether the employee must pay, whether insurance applies, or when replacement equipment will arrive.

3.3 OPTIONAL ACCESSORIES
The standard kit is a laptop, charger, and headset. A second monitor, specialist input device, or other accessory requires the manager's approval and IT availability review. Explain the work requirement in the request. Remote status or travel plans alone do not establish eligibility.

If a workplace adjustment is needed, use the confidential Workplace Services process and involve IT for device compatibility. Do not put medical details into an ordinary accessory request or a general assistant chat. Approval of an adjustment does not mean a particular device is in stock.

3.4 RETURN OR REPLACEMENT
Wait for IT's instructions before shipping or handing over a device. For office handover, confirm the location and entry process. For remote return, use the secure collection instructions supplied by IT. Do not infer a shipping address from an office name.

Keep the handover acknowledgement. Sending a device is not proof that IT has received it or issued a replacement. Ask IT about approved data-handling steps; do not independently erase the device or copy company data to personal storage.

4. SUPPORT AND ESCALATION
IT Support owns faults, returns, and replacement review. The manager coordinates work impact. Workplace Services handles access and confidential workplace-adjustment coordination.
